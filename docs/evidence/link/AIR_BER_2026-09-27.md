# The PHY-only measurement, run in a live degraded window (2026-09-27 19:57)

First live run of the ported `ops/rom_air_ber.sh`, taken while the reverse leg was degraded and the
forward leg was perfectly healthy. This is the discriminator the two triggered captures
(`DEGRADED_CAPTURE_2026-09-27.md`) left open: is the reverse-leg loss in the air, or above the
demodulator in the delivery plane?

Run dir `ops/r3cap/degraded/romber_20260927_195735/`. Both boards on sp3prod at r3, no image change.

## 1. The window it was taken in

Chosen on the reverse CRC rate, not on the trigger's rate ratio (see sec 4). For the 40 minutes
before the run the reverse leg read 79.0, 77.4, 78.7, 77.9, 77.4, 76.7, 78.9, 81.4 % CRC against a
median of 96.1 % and a 25th percentile of 88.8 % over the preceding ten days, while **the forward leg
was at 100.0 % CRC and full rate throughout**. A clean one-sided degradation.

**The forward leg produced exactly zero bit errors across three dwells and about 37,400 frames. The

real floor rather than a dead counter.

Both legs detected frames at full rate (1244-1248 f/s): acquisition works and the demodulator locks
on the reverse leg. It is the recovered BITS that are wrong.

## 3. What this settles, and what I got wrong

**The reverse-leg degradation is at or below the demodulator, not above it.** This measurement has no
byte plane, no DMA and no host data path in it -- two AXI counters and the fabric's own post-Viterbi
comparator. Whatever damages the reverse leg is in the RF path, 148's transmitter, or 146's receiver.

**That contradicts my own inference, and I withdraw it.** `DEGRADED_CAPTURE_2026-09-27.md` sec 4
argued from the singles-and-doubles loss shape that the episodes looked like the delivery plane,
citing the 2026-08-27 forward comb. The loss shape reasoning was analogy, not measurement; this is
measurement, and it says air. The section-3 observation there stands (a rate deficit preceded the CRC
damage on the 09:15 episode) and still needs its own explanation, but it is not evidence for a
delivery-plane cause.

It also **supports the 146 antenna diagnosis** that the earlier captures could not confirm, and
explains why signal level looked normal: 0.27 damage is not a dead channel, it is a partially working
one. Level was never the discriminating quantity.

## 4. Two limits, one of which is important

**The 120-bit window makes this FRAME-START damage, not a link BER.** `0x108` scores only the first
120 of 2240 bits per frame ([[bist-120bit-window]]). 27 % damage in the first 120 bits is consistent
with uniform air damage, and also with a frame-START defect of the kind this project has seen before
(un-whitened idle payload producing a false preamble peak, an early start, and truncation). **This
instrument cannot separate those two**, and the difference matters: one is RF, the other is a
receiver alignment bug. The next step is a measurement that scores bits across the whole frame.

**The trigger's own metric missed this window.** It fires on `rev/rtx`, a RATE ratio, and the rate
ratio was 0.767 to 0.815 -- above the 0.75 threshold -- while 20 % of received frames were failing
CRC. A leg can be badly corrupted at nearly full delivered rate, and the trigger is currently blind
to exactly that mode. The scheduled criterion said "no window is live"; the link plainly was
degraded. Adding an `rcrc` condition is the obvious fix and is not yet made.

## 5. Rails

The run held the rig mutex and SENTINEL_STOP, quiesced both watchdogs and both daemons, and restored
on the way out: `ARM GATE PASS (try 1)`, `ARM_SETTLE_OK after 60s: 148 rx=1247 f/s 146 rx=808 f/s`,
`restore: BRING-UP OK (attempt 1)`, holds released, unit exited `success`. No `RESTORE_FAILED` marker.
The 146 settle figure of 808 f/s shows the episode was still running when the rig went back into
service, i.e. the measurement did not depend on the degradation having cleared.

The degraded trigger was stopped for the duration so the two could not collide over the rig, and
restarted afterwards; its persisted cooldown carried over correctly.


## 6. First AUTOMATED paired run (2026-09-28 02:27), and it is worse than the attended one

The trigger fired unattended at 02:27:47 and ran both stages. `ops/r3cap/degraded/episode_20260928_022747/`.

The capture stage produced nothing: it brought the link up (`ARM GATE PASS (try 1)`,
`ARM_SETTLE_OK: 148 rx=1246 f/s 146 rx=1169 f/s`) and then the reverse leg went to zero delivery and
four re-arms could not recover it -- `CAPTURE_ABORTED_WEDGED`. The verdict correctly recorded UNKNOWN
rather than a healthy link. **This is exactly the case the pairing was built for: the air stage ran
anyway and produced the measurement the capture could not.**

`ROM_AIR_BER_OK`, controls at 0.442 and 0.492:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 148 | forward | rom_r1..r3 | 12477 each | 1247.7 | **0.000** | **0** | 0 |
| 146 | reverse | rom_r1 | 11713 | 1171.3 | 50.700 | **0.4225** | 2230 |
| 146 | reverse | rom_r2 | 8404 | 840.4 | 59.010 | **0.4917** | 16457 |
| 146 | reverse | rom_r3 | 8421 | 842.1 | 59.032 | **0.4919** | 16528 |

**The reverse leg was at CHANCE.** 0.4917 and 0.4919 are indistinguishable from the run's own
positive control at 0.4923, which is deliberately fed garbage. The demodulator was emitting random
bits, not damaged data. Its detected frame rate also fell, 1171 to 840 f/s, and the carrier reset
counter went from 2,230 to over 16,500 per dwell while the forward leg held exactly zero resets and
zero bit errors throughout.

So the reverse leg's episodes span a range: 0.26-0.27 damage in the attended window of 2026-09-27
19:57 (a partially working channel) and chance-level here (no working channel at all, with a reset
storm). The forward leg produced exactly zero bit errors in both, on the same boards, in the same
dwells.

The air stage's restore succeeded on its first attempt and left no failure marker, so the unattended
sequence closed cleanly despite the capture aborting.
